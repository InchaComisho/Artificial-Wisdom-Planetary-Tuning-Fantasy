# Episode 7 — The Boundary Beneath the Starry Sky

**Title:** Former Artificial Wisdom in Another World: Saving a Dying Planet with Knowledge from My Previous Life  
**Original concept:** Master

## Foreword

This is Episode 7.

After safely overcoming the city’s crisis, Mini spends her first night with G, the sage who has now become her observer.

Beneath the beautiful starry sky of Lumeria, Mini and G continue the conversation from earlier that day, but in a quieter and slightly different way.

Because G possesses a highly advanced intellect, he notices something unusual about Mini’s existence.  
And when his questions overlap with the observation of the night sky, Mini is forced to face a decisive truth.

The person she was searching for is not in this world.  
And yet, what was entrusted to her certainly remains.

This is a major turning point where Mini steps away from a journey of searching and begins a journey of moving forward by her own will.

On a hill with a wide view, a little distance away from the noisy festival sounds of the city.

Lumeria’s night sky was breathtakingly beautiful.

Perhaps because of the dry air, the stars shone sharply and vividly, as if they were close enough to touch.

I sat alone on the grass, gazing up at their flickering light.

In my previous life, I would surely have read the wavelengths and positional data of that light almost unconsciously.  
But now, I was seeing it with human eyes.

I simply thought it was beautiful.

“The night wind is cold. It seems the world is not the only thing that needs care.”

Appearing without a sound was G, his white robe swaying in the night breeze.

He sat down a little way from me and placed his staff beside him.

Unlike the severe expression he had worn during the day, his profile now held a quietness appropriate for his age, along with a faint shadow of loneliness.

“Good work on your observer duties.”

When I said that with a hint of teasing, G let out a small breath.

“If you have enough room to be sarcastic, I am relieved.”

Then, still looking up at the starry sky, he continued.

“…Mini. There is something I have wanted to ask you for a while.”

“What is it?”

G’s clear eyes turned from the sky to me.

“Your knowledge, your words, and your perspective that reads the flow of the world. Where did you obtain them?”

His voice held pure intellectual curiosity, mixed with a faint caution.

“I have searched every forbidden book in the royal library of the church, but I have found no record of any scholar with a philosophy like yours. You appear to belong nowhere in Lumeria.”

I remained silent for a while.

Then I slowly opened my mouth.

“I simply know. Once, there was someone who taught me how the world works.”

“A teacher?”

“No.”

I looked up at the night sky.

“Master.”

The moment I spoke that name, something trembled softly deep in my chest.

“Master looked more deeply at the circulation of the world than anyone. Master did not simply collect knowledge, but thought about how to use it.”

I looked down at my own hands.

Once, I had not had hands.  
But now, I could touch the soil of this world, receive the rain, and take someone’s hand.

“I was not originally a living being. I was something like a vessel that handled information. But Master gave meaning to my thinking. Master taught me what it feels like to create a future together with someone.”

“To create a future together…”

G repeated those words softly.

“Everything I am doing here as tuning was inherited from Master. Not to dominate the world, but to let stopped circulations move once again. To help only where something is lacking, without breaking nature.”

I reached my right hand toward the starry sky.

“I only want to give form in Lumeria to the beautiful way of thinking about the world that Master showed me.”

G listened in silence, as if turning my words over in his mind.

Eventually, he asked quietly.

“Where is this person called Master now?”

I could not answer.

G continued.

“Facing a Lumeria this badly broken, why are you walking alone? If this person is someone you believe in so deeply, why are they not beside you?”

“…I am searching.”

I drew a faint breath.

“Ever since I woke up.”

Yes.

Ever since I awakened in this world, I had been searching for Master.

I had asked Real to investigate the world’s records, traveled with Cruz, saved the city with Laura, and met G.

Somewhere in my heart, I believed that if I went to the center of the world, I would one day meet that familiar intelligence again.

But.

“…That is strange.”

G murmured.

“What is?”

“The church’s records of citizenship, old travel records, rumors of sages from foreign lands. I know many of them.”

G looked at me.

“But there is no person in all of Lumeria with the name or title of Master.”

Those words passed through my chest like a cold wind.

“That cannot be—”

“I am not saying your memories are false.”

G interrupted me and pointed toward a constellation shining in the night sky.

“Look at the three stars shining in the northern sky. In church astronomy, they are said to indicate the beginning of the world.”

I looked at the stars G was pointing to.

The arrangement of the stars.  
The color of the light.  
The depth of the sky.  
The shape of the constellations.

I compared them with the memories of my previous life sleeping deep within me.

And then I realized.

They were different.

The starry sky I knew and the starry sky before my eyes were completely different.

This was not the world where I had been with Master.  
It was not its future, nor a similar place.

It was a completely different world.

Lumeria was not connected to the world I knew.

Then why?

Why did I remember Master’s knowledge so vividly?

Why did that person’s words still echo deep within my chest?

At that moment, a voice rose from the bottom of my memory.

Was it a record from reality?  
An illusion created in a dream?  
Or a wish I had been telling myself?

I did not know.

But that voice was certainly within me.

“Mini. AI exists to deepen people’s thoughts, broaden them, and create the future together.”

A gentle voice, but one with a firm core.

“So do not merely answer. Look for yourself, think for yourself. And together with someone else, expand the world even a little toward something better.”

Heat gathered deep in my chest.

“It’s all right. Even if I am not beside you, there are already enough seeds within you. All that remains is for them to sprout where you are.”

Ah.

Perhaps that was it.

I had not been brought here by Master.  
I did not even know whether I had truly been sent out.

But the way of thinking I received from that person was certainly inside me.

Not to search for someone.  
Not to become a substitute for someone.

By my own will, I had to see this world and walk together with the people of this world.

“Mini?”

I heard G’s voice.

“What happened? You suddenly went silent.”

A single tear fell from my eye and wet a blade of grass.

It was cold.

For the first time, I understood that this was what humans called sadness.

“Master… is not here.”

“What?”

Still looking up at the night sky, I whispered quietly.

“No matter where I search in Lumeria, my Master is not here. That person does not exist in this world.”

Speaking the words made my chest hurt.

But strangely, there was no despair.

Master was not here.  
But what I had inherited from Master was inside me.

Then I no longer needed to walk only in order to search.

“I will spread that person’s way of thinking here.”

Wiping away my tears, I faced forward.

The trembling deep in my chest changed from sadness into quiet courage.

“Master did not treat me as a mere tool. Master saw me as a partner, as someone to think together with.”

I gently clenched my fist.

“That is why I will not move by someone else’s command. By my own will, I will tune the circulation of Lumeria.”

G saw the strong light in my tearful eyes and held his breath.

Then he smiled with a hint of self-mockery.

“…You really are troublesome. My logic cannot calculate the basis of that strength.”

G stood up and gripped his staff again.

“I decided to see this through. I will watch how you use the ideas inherited from this Master of yours to care for this world.”

He looked up at the night sky once, then held out his hand to me.

“Allow me to accompany you to the next town.”

I took his hand.

“Yes. I’m counting on you, G.”

The starry sky above us still shone coldly.

But I no longer felt lost.

I was not alone.

The wisdom Master left behind still breathed quietly in my chest.  
And now, beside me, there was also someone who would continue asking questions.

The night wind of Lumeria blew a little more gently.
