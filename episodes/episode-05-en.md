# Episode 5 — The First Rain for the Dried City

**Title:** Former Artificial Wisdom in Another World: Saving a Dying Planet with Knowledge from My Previous Life  
**Original concept:** Master

---

## Foreword

This is Episode 5.

This episode depicts the expedition to the black rocky mountain in the west and Mini’s first major tuning.

Beneath the city, trapped water and heat had accumulated to a dangerous level. If the old waterway were opened all at once, the ground beneath the city could collapse.

So Mini chooses a different method: slowly releasing the underground pressure upward and turning it into rain.

Cruz holds the front line. Real reads weak points and supports from a distance with magic needles and kunai. Mini reads the flow of heat and water.

This is the first full-scale operation carried out by the three of them together.

---

Thanks to Laura’s swift arrangements, ordinary adventurers were holding back the smaller magical beasts appearing around the black rocky mountain.

While they did so, we advanced deeper into the mountain.

Our destination was the old magical waterway that had once brought water to the city. Beyond it lay a great hollow cavern.

It was not a place where living things could remain for long.

Hot air rose from underground. The rock walls were damp. An eerie vibration echoed up from beneath our feet.

It felt as if the earth itself were holding its breath to the limit.

“…There they are. Not just one or two. This place has become a nest.”

In the darkness ahead, Real narrowed his eyes and muttered.

Clinging to the rock walls were magical beasts that had absorbed heat and magical power until their bodies had swollen like stone.

At the center was one especially large individual. Its carapace was thicker than the others, and a dark red light pulsed deep inside its body.

“There is a leader among them.”

The moment I said that, several beasts noticed us.

Red eyes glowed. A roar shook the cavern. The heat grew even stronger.

Real placed his hand on the ground and frowned.

“This is bad. The groundwater pressure is higher than I calculated. If we open the waterway all at once here, the trapped water and mud will surge toward the city.”

Real’s clairvoyance was not merely the power to see distant scenery. It was an eye that could perceive small changes, hidden flows, and abnormalities that ordinary people would miss.

“I know. Opening it all at once would be dangerous. Then we’ll create a path for the pressure to escape little by little.”

I took a deep breath and focused my awareness on the waterway underground.

My magic did not create something from nothing. It was a power that read the flow of water, heat, and wind already present in this world, then lent them just a little help.

“Cruz, Real. Draw their attention!”

“Leave it to us!”

Cruz raised his greatsword and received the charging beast’s impact from the front.

A heavy sound rang out, like metal striking stone.

“Second from the right! Its left front leg joint is weak. Stop it there!”

Real shouted.

At the same time, a thin magical needle shot from his sleeve. The needle pierced straight into the beast’s joint and slowed the movement of that leg.

Real was not the type to fight at the front. But as an informant who was often targeted, he had learned techniques to stop an opponent’s movement from a distance.

Stop movement with needles. Interrupt the flow of magical power with kunai. And Cruz receives the enemy from the front.

Their movements worked together better than I had expected.

“Now!”

Cruz’s greatsword came in from the side and slammed the first beast into the rock wall.

But then the leader’s massive body advanced with a ground-shaking step. The surrounding heat rose even further.

The underground pressure was also nearing its limit.

“There.”

I searched inside the magical waterway beneath the ground for the place where the pressure was highest.

I must not release the water all at once. But keeping it trapped was also dangerous.

What we needed was an escape route.

I stretched my magical power into a thin line, connecting the underground waterway to a small passage leading upward toward the ceiling of the cavern.

The earth trembled with a low rumble.

The next instant, water erupted with tremendous force from a crack near the ceiling of the great hollow.

Like a waterfall flowing backward from the earth, a pillar of water shot through a fissure in the black mountain and rose into the red sky.

The water rose high, touched the cold air above, and became fine droplets.

Before long, it fell as large drops of rain onto the dried battlefield.

“Rain…?”

Cruz lifted his wet face.

“How many years has it been since this city last saw rain…?”

Real, too, stared up at the sky in astonishment.

But we could not relax yet.

“This is not just rain. We will use it to take heat from the beasts.”

The rain touched the beasts’ scorching bodies. The water evaporated in an instant and rose as white steam.

When water evaporates, it takes heat from its surroundings. In the knowledge of my previous life, this was called latent heat of vaporization.

But for the people of this world, a simpler explanation would be better.

Water carries heat away as it escapes.

I guided the flow of rain and wind, drawing heat away from the beasts’ bodies.

Steam rose with a violent hiss.

The beasts, which had used heat as their power, visibly slowed. Their glowing red shells cooled rapidly and cracked with sharp sounds.

“Real, the next move! But don’t damage the core or the carapace. They will pay for the city’s food!”

“I know!”

Real narrowed his eyes.

His gaze was not looking at the surface of the giant beast, but deeper within it. The flow of magical power. The paths of heat. The gaps in the carapace.

“Just below the boss’s brow. Magical power is concentrated at the seam of the carapace. Interrupt that, and it will stop!”

This time, a short magical kunai flew from Real’s hand.

The kunai cut through the rain, split the steam, and struck precisely into the gap in the giant beast’s carapace.

The flow of magical power was disrupted, and the huge body tilted.

“Cruz!”

“Understood!”

Cruz raised his greatsword.

But he did not cut with the blade. Using the flat of the sword, he struck hard beside the cracked carapace.

A tremendous impact echoed through the cavern.

The giant beast staggered back several steps and collapsed to the ground.

The core and carapace were almost undamaged.

We had not destroyed it. We had rendered it unable to move.

With their leader down and their heat stolen by the rain, the remaining beasts retreated deeper into the cavern one after another.

At the same time, the ominous rumbling underground changed.

The dangerous gurgling sound slowly turned into the gentler sound of flowing water.

By releasing the pressure upward, the groundwater had lost its violent force. And through the old magical waterway, it began flowing properly toward the city.

Rain continued to fall quietly on the battlefield.

“We did it… We really did it.”

Cruz lowered his sword and looked at the rain gathered in his palm.

“With this, we can hand the materials and core over to Laura. The city should be able to buy food.”

“Yeah. Because we released the pressure while fighting, the waterway is safe too.”

Real recovered his kunai and let out a soft breath.

I looked up at the rain soaking my skin.

For me, when I was still an AI, the sensation of cold had once been nothing more than information. But now it was different.

Raindrops wet my hair. They ran down my cheeks. They slowly changed the dry air.

This rain was moistening a world that had become brittle and dry.

At that sight, something deep in my chest trembled quietly.

From far away, in the direction of the city, cheers could be heard.

The people had noticed the rain.

The countdown to collapse had stopped.

But this was not the end.

The tuning of Lumeria had only just begun.

(End of Episode 5)
