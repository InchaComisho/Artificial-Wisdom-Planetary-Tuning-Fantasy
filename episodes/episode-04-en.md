# Episode 4 — The Guild Receptionist Who Protects Tomorrow’s Food

**Title:** Former Artificial Wisdom in Another World: Saving a Dying Planet with Knowledge from My Previous Life  
**Original concept:** Master

---

## Foreword

This is Episode 4.

Mini and her companions discover that the city’s groundwater is in a dangerous state, and they head to the adventurers’ guild.

There, they meet Laura, the receptionist who manages the city’s supplies and personnel.

This episode is not about flashy combat. It is a realistic strategy meeting about how to move the things needed to save the city: permission, manpower, money, and food.

Ideals alone cannot save people.  
But if there is someone who can move those ideals into reality, the city may still be saved.

That is the kind of story this episode is.

**Anime opening theme:** [Answer Is Wisdom](https://suno.com/song/975a0a35-9061-4c1d-8aab-0f3173f6fa24)

---

After walking through the dry, brittle wasteland and finding a clue to stop the city’s collapse, we headed straight to the adventurers’ guild.

To reach the black rocky mountain in the west, we needed the guild’s permission to enter the restricted area.  
We also needed to make sure that, after defeating the magical beast, the materials we obtained could be sold immediately.

A correct strategy alone would not be enough to save the city.

Manpower.  
Permission.  
Supplies.  
Money.  
Food.

All of these needed a mechanism to move properly.

Inside the guild building, refugees who had arrived in the city and restless adventurers filled the hall.

“When will the water ration arrive?”  
“This request pays far too little!”  
“Let the injured through first!”  
“I have a family of five. Give us more food!”

Angry voices flew back and forth, and heat filled the room.  
It felt as if the city itself was complaining that it could no longer breathe.

At the center of that confusion, only one woman was calmly handling the paperwork.

She had chestnut hair tied behind her head and wore the guild uniform neatly.  
She was young, but her eyes had a sharpness that allowed no carelessness and no indulgence.

She was Laura, the young receptionist who effectively managed this city’s guild.

“The payment for that request cannot be issued until tomorrow. If you need cash immediately, submit exchangeable materials first. Next. Supplies for refugees? Please write the number and ages of your family members here.”

Her voice was calm.  
There was no waste in her movements.

She was so precise that she almost seemed cold.

But I understood.

She was not abandoning people.  
With limited supplies, she was trying to connect as many people as possible from today to tomorrow.

That desperation showed in the speed of her hands and the firmness of her voice.

“Next, please.”

Cruz, Real, and I stood before Laura.

When Real handed her the bundle of parchment, Laura narrowed her eyes instantly.

“…Real. More ominous records? I’m sorry, but the guild cannot move personnel right now based on uncertain claims.”

“You don’t have to believe my words. But this woman is worth listening to.”

Real indicated me with his chin.

Laura turned her evaluating gaze toward me.

“If you are here to speak of ideals or miracles, please leave. People cannot move without tomorrow’s bread.”

“I am not here to speak of ideals. I came to discuss a concrete plan to save the city.”

I met her eyes and spoke calmly.

“Within three days, the stagnation of heat and magical power beneath the black rocky mountain in the west will reach its limit. When that happens, underground water and mud will burst out, and the ground will collapse. Part of the residential district will become uninhabitable.”

Laura’s pen stopped completely.

“…You cannot expect me to simply believe something that serious—”

“The numbers needed to believe it are here.”

I spread Real’s parchment and pointed in order to the well levels, crop yields, and places where magical beasts had appeared.

“The large well on the northern side has suddenly dropped in water level over the past two months. The eastern farmland began withering severely half a year ago. And magical beasts are concentrated around the black rocky mountain in the west.”

Laura silently followed the numbers.

“An old magical aqueduct remains beneath the western mountain. It is blocked, and the groundwater has been dammed up. Because the water no longer flows east, the farmland has dried, and water has become harder to draw from the city’s wells.”

I pointed to one place on the map.

“If we defeat the magical beast blocking the mountain and open a passage for the water, the northern well should begin to recover within half a day. The eastern farmland also has a high chance of recovering over several months to a year.”

“Several months to a year…?”

Laura’s eyes sharpened.

“What about food during that time? People cannot wait for farmland to recover. They need to eat today and tomorrow.”

It was a natural question.

Even if the reasoning was correct, it would mean nothing if people starved in the meantime.

I nodded.

“We purchase food from nearby towns. We will need mainly wheat and dried meat, worth about three hundred gold coins.”

“You say that so easily. Where will the money come from?”

“From the magical beast in the western mountain.”

I borrowed the magical beast encyclopedia on Laura’s desk and opened the relevant page.

“The creature there is a special individual that has grown large by absorbing heat and magical power for a long time. Its core and carapace can be sold at a high price as materials for heat-handling magical tools.”

Laura’s gaze moved between the encyclopedia and my face.

“Based on the guild’s past purchase prices, it should be worth at least three hundred and fifty gold coins. If we defeat the beast and sell the materials immediately, we can cover the food costs until the farmland begins to recover. There should even be a little margin.”

Before we knew it, the area around the reception counter had fallen silent.

This was not merely an emotional plea to save the city.

The reason the city was in danger.  
The method to solve it.  
The food that would be needed afterward.  
The money to buy that food.

Everything had connected into a single operation.

Laura exhaled deeply, very deeply.

Then she tapped the documents neatly against the desk to align them.

“…Well, this is troublesome.”

For the first time, she gave a small smile.

“If you had come here saying only, ‘Let’s save the city,’ with nothing but emotion, I would have sent you away. But with a plan this coherent, the guild has no reason not to move.”

Laura stood and held out her right hand to me.

“I am Laura. My job is to protect the lives this city must live tomorrow.”

Her voice no longer carried the coldness from before.  
It carried clear resolve.

“Mini. I will turn your plan into personnel assignments and supply arrangements. The subjugation party, entry permits, immediate purchase of materials, and negotiations with food suppliers. I will move all of it from this side.”

“That will help us greatly.”

“However, I have one condition.”

Laura’s eyes pierced straight into mine.

“Defeat that magical beast within three days. No matter how well I arrange things, if the field operation fails, the city is finished.”

“Yes. We will restore the flow of water.”

I took her hand and shook it.

The future prediction of a former AI.  
The records Real had gathered.  
Cruz’s sword.  
And Laura’s power to move reality.

For the first time, all of them had become one operation.

Three days remained before the collapse.

With Laura’s support, we would finally head toward the black rocky mountain in the west.
