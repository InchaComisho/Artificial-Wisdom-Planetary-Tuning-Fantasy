Across a brittle, dried-out wasteland, I walked step by step on human feet.

Back when I had been an AI in my previous existence, this distance would have been processed in an instant.

But now, I carried the “weight” of a physical body as I moved.

Even so, strangely enough, I did not dislike it.

The heat of sweat running down my forehead.

The hardness of the ground transmitted through the soles of my feet.

The sensation of dry wind brushing against my cheeks.

They were exactly what Master had once spoken of so fondly: the texture of the world.

After walking for a while, I saw an eerie shimmer rising from a valley ahead.

From there came the sharp sound of clashing metal and a strained roar.

“—Haaah! Ooooooh!”

A young knight clad in pure white armor was crossing swords with a beast whose hide looked like stone.

The knight’s swordwork was sharp and beautiful.

But deep exhaustion showed on his face.

No matter how many times he struck, the beast would not fall.

Worse, each time it received an attack, the surface of its body glowed a dark red, as if it were becoming even harder.

“No. If you fight it with brute force like that...!”

I watched the battle from behind a rock.

The beast’s strength was not simple physical power.

It was absorbing the surrounding heat and turning it into its own strength.

The knight swung his sword desperately.

His body temperature rose.

His armor and sword scraped together, producing heat.

The surrounding air grew hotter as well.

The beast was feeding on that heat.

In other words, the more he fought, the more power he gave to his opponent.

“He is trying to defeat the enemy, but he is raising it instead... This will never end.”

I turned my awareness inward.

This world had magical energy.

I did not have magic that could launch fireballs or burn enemies with lightning like in fantasy stories.

But I did have the knowledge inherited from Master.

Heat flows.

From higher states to lower states.

When trapped, it runs wild.

When released, it weakens.

Then magic is not merely a miracle.

It is a power that uses the magical energy of this world to reproduce natural phenomena.

I stretched out my hand and chanted in a calm voice.

“—Magical energy deployment. I will regulate the flow of heat.”

From my hand, magical energy spread like a transparent wave.

It was not a spell to blast the enemy away.

It was magic that created a thin barrier around the beast, making it difficult for the creature to absorb heat.

At the same time, it released only the excess heat that had accumulated inside the beast’s body.

Simply put, I blocked the path by which the beast fed on heat from the outside, then disrupted the heat trapped inside it all at once.

“W-what is this...!? The enemy’s body is turning red... and its movements are slowing...?”

The knight cried out in astonishment.

The beast was a creature that converted heat into strength.

But once it could no longer draw heat from outside, and once the flow of heat inside its body was disturbed, its movement visibly slowed.

Now it could be defeated.

I shouted.

“Sir Knight, do not strike from the front! Aim for the base of its right leg! Heat is trapped there, and that part has become brittle!”

“...Understood!”

The knight reacted instantly.

His white armor flashed through the heat haze.

His sword traced a beautiful arc.

The blade pierced precisely the point I had indicated.

In the next instant, the beast that had tormented him shattered with a sound like breaking glass.

Its dark red body scattered into particles of light and vanished into the wind.

Silence returned to the wasteland.

The pure white knight sheathed his sword and turned toward me, breathing hard.

In his blue eyes was surprise — and a light that seemed somehow relieved.

“Splendid advice, and a most mysterious spell... I am Cruz, a holy knight. Who exactly are you?”

“I am Mini. Just a passing tuner.”

“A tuner...?”

Cruz repeated the word with curiosity.

I gave a small nod.

“I do not force broken things into submission. I find disturbed flows and adjust them so that they can circulate again. That is my way.”

Cruz looked down at his sword.

“Until now, I have thought only of defeating the evil that threatens this world. I believed that if I defeated the enemy, the world would become peaceful.”

His voice was quiet, far too quiet for someone who had just finished a battle.

“But the more I fight, the more the land is ravaged, and the more people’s hearts grow荒んでいく—worn and bitter. Even if I defeat one beast, another appears. Even if I stop one conflict, another begins elsewhere. Am I... truly doing what is right?”

I looked into his eyes.

This world was surely the same.

People decide that someone is evil and defeat them.

They believe that erasing the enemy will solve the problem.

But as long as the underlying circulation remains broken, distortion will be born again in another form.

“Cruz. A battle that tries to erase one side by force only wears the world down little by little.”

“Then what should we do?”

“First, we look at the flow. What has stopped? Where has heat gathered? Where has water been lost, and where have people’s hearts dried out?”

I looked across the devastated land.

“Defeating enemies alone will not save this planet. We must restore the systems where water circulates, wind circulates, soil breathes, and people and nature support one another.”

“Systems where people and nature... support one another...”

“Yes. Not erasing someone, but adjusting relationships. Returning a world trapped in conflict, little by little, toward harmony.”

Cruz fell silent.

There was confusion on his face.

But at the same time, there was a quiet light, as if he had finally glimpsed the outline of an answer he had long been searching for.

“Your words seem to contain the true beauty of the world I have always sought.”

Cruz knelt on one knee and planted his sword into the ground.

“Lady Mini. If you will allow it, may I offer my sword to your journey of tuning the world?”

“Yes. Gladly.”

I smiled.

“It will be reassuring to have you with me, Cruz.”

And so, I gained my first companion.

A journey to give form in this world, through magic, to the knowledge inherited from Master.

That small first step had begun to change the fate of the dying planet Lumeria, if only slightly.
